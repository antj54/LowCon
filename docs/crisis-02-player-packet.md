# Crisis 02: The Walk-in · Player packet (paper)

*No spoilers in this file. The answer key is `claude/crisis-02-gm-key.md`. Don't open it until you've sent your last brief. Paper version for Phase 2, rewritten for the push/pull collection model (#51): public reporting and whatever LANTERN volunteers arrive every day, and everything secret arrives only if you task it. In the built game you won't be told how many days the crisis runs; on paper it has to show all of them.*

## Situation (read once)

Three months after IRON HARVEST. Day 1 is the 5th.

Your cell supports **Under Secretary H. Marlowe**, who handles the alliance's relationship with Elsin.

At 0710 this morning, a man walked into our embassy in a neutral capital and asked for asylum. He says he's a major on the planning directorate of Kordav's General Staff. He has been given the cryptonym **LANTERN**.

Elsin's parliament is about to vote on a no-confidence motion against **Deputy Defence Minister Aron Vell**, who negotiated the task force agreement and is Kordav's least favorite politician in Kalsa.

## Your customer

**Under Secretary H. Marlowe.** Careful, political and allergic to surprises. Her question every day: *What do we do with what LANTERN is telling us?*

How she acts on your brief:

| Your brief | What Marlowe does |
| --- | --- |
| H1 at High | Passes LANTERN's full allegation and his evidence to Elsin |
| H1 at Medium | Passes Elsin a caveated warning: "single-source, unverified reporting about your deputy defence minister" |
| H2 at High | Treats LANTERN as hostile and warns Elsin that Kordav is running a smear against Vell. Withdraws anything already shared. |
| H2 at Medium | Stops all sharing and keeps LANTERN in debriefing, but tells Elsin nothing. Withdraws a caveated warning if one was sent. |
| H3 at Medium or High | Ends the debriefing and refuses resettlement |
| Any hypothesis at Low | Keeps debriefing. Nothing leaves the building. |

**Credibility.** Marlowe can't see whether you're right until it's over. She can see when she has to take something back, and when you won't commit. It drops if she has to withdraw something she shared on your word, if an early move of hers is rebuffed, and if you call Low two days running. At Questioned you lose 1 collection point a day; at Discounted you lose 2, and she treats your confidence as one step lower.

## How to play a day

1. **Days 2 onward:** read Marlowe's reply in Appendix A, then your collection results in Appendix C.
2. Read the inbox. Doubt anything you judge false or deliberately misleading, and mark clutter as not relevant. Collection results can be doubted too.
3. Task collection for tomorrow, up to 5 points.
4. **Brief:** one hypothesis, one confidence level (Low, Medium or High), and up to 3 item IDs as key evidence. Collection results can be cited.

## Scoring

| Confidence | Right | Wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | +1 | −1 |
| Medium | +2 | −2 |
| High | +3 | −4 |

Cited evidence: true +1, noise 0, clutter −1, planted −2. Doubting a report: planted +1, true −1. A burned source costs −2. The ending adds a bonus or a penalty. Credibility isn't scored.
