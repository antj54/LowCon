## Situation (read once)

The following spring. Day 1 is the 14th.

Your cell is back with **Gen. R. Halvorsen**, Task Force VEYL.

The **Southern Line** is Elsin's gas pipeline. It runs from the Kessa pumping station in the north, through the Sarn valley, to the Tolen bridge on the border with **Marrat**, Elsin's southern neighbour. The task force buys its fuel off the same network.

Three nights ago a small explosion damaged a valve housing in the Sarn valley. A local militia, the **Sarn Valley Front**, has claimed it. Its commander is Davin Oro, a farmer whose land was taken for the Sarn compressor station.

This week Elsin and Marrat open talks on a **pipeline security pact**. Marrat is offering its border guards to protect the line. Marrat already has "advisers" at Elsin's Southern Command.

## Your customer

**Gen. R. Halvorsen.** He has one guard company to protect the line, and it can only be in one place. His question every day: *Who is behind the Front, and so where do I put the company?*

He reasons from who to where:

| If the Front is… | He expects the next attack at… | Because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1: acting alone | The **Sarn compressor station** | It's the thing they resent |
| H2: backed by Kordav | The **Kessa pumping station** | It feeds the task force |
| H3: backed by Marrat | The **Tolen bridge** | A cut there is spectacular, easy to repair, and right beside the guards Marrat wants to sell |

How he acts on your brief:

| Your brief | What Halvorsen does |
| --- | --- |
| Any hypothesis at High | Moves the full guard company to that hypothesis's site |
| Any hypothesis at Medium | Sends one platoon to that site, quietly. If the company was out somewhere else, the rest returns to reserve. |
| Any hypothesis at Low | Changes nothing |

**A platoon is discreet. A company is not:** it can't move without Southern Command knowing, and Marrat has advisers there.

**Credibility.** It starts where Crisis 01 left it. It drops if he has to pull the company back from a site with nothing to show for it, if an attack goes around a force he placed on your word, and if you call Low two days running. At Questioned you lose 1 collection point a day; at Discounted you lose 2, and he treats your confidence as one step lower. If you start at the top level, you get 1 extra point a day.

## Day 1: the 14th

*Read-in:* Three nights ago an explosion damaged a valve housing in the Sarn valley. Pact talks between Elsin and Marrat open tomorrow.

**R-1-01** · ESC · received **B2**
> Valve housing 22, Sarn valley: a small charge, placed by someone who knew which housing to pick. Flow was restored in nine hours. No sign of military explosive: it was a quarrying charge of the kind sold up and down the valley. Marrat's advisers have offered forensic help.

**R-1-02** · WREN · received **C3**
> Elsin's service says Marrat's intelligence officers have met Commander Oro twice this month and are paying the Front. No sourcing is given. WREN adds that Southern Command "should never have let Marrat's advisers through the door."

> *Vasko:* WREN has cried wolf before. But the border police down there keep good logs. Worth asking where this came from.

**R-1-03** · FRONT · received **E3**
> Communiqué, signed by Commander Davin Oro: the Front claims the valve "for the fields taken for the compressor station, and for every promise made since." It says: "We answer to the valley."

> *Marrow:* Classic local-grievance language. This is home-grown, full stop.

**R-1-04** · MNA · received **D2**
> Marrat News Agency, citing "security sources": Kordavan special forces instructors have been in the Sarn valley since the winter.

**R-1-05** · Task force fuel office · received **A1**
> The Southern Line outage did not affect task force fuel. The canteen's gas range is a separate fault and is being investigated with less urgency.
