## Situation (read once)

The 15th of November. Two years ago this week, your cell watched an exercise at Ostrel.

Kordav's note expires at dawn on the 20th.

**If there is an agreement in principle:** on the 20th the task force is to hand its forward posts in the Tarsk sector to Elsin's border police and pull back to garrison, while Kordav's 4th Army returns to barracks. Both sides have observers at Dolan.

**If there is none:** 4th Army is still at Ostrel, with its reservists. Kordav says only that the note "means what it says."
