## Situation (read once)

Day 1 is the 15th, early 2030s.

You lead the analysis cell supporting **Task Force VEYL**, a multinational reassurance force based in Elsin.

Kordav and Elsin have been arguing over the Veyl River for ten years. The river starts in Elsin's highlands and feeds Kordav's farmland. Elsin's **Tarsk Dam**, 6 km inside the border, controls the flow. Water talks open tomorrow (Day 2) in a neutral capital.

Two days ago Kordav's 4th Army started its annual autumn exercise, **IRON HARVEST**, at the **Ostrel training area**, 40 km from the border. The announced end date is **the 20th** (the day after Day 5).

## Your customer

**Gen. R. Halvorsen**, commander of Task Force VEYL. He wants a yes or no: does he move **2nd Battalion** to the Tarsk sector? An early move is visible, and Kordav will call it provocation in the middle of talks. A late move might not arrive in time.

How he acts on your brief:

| Your brief | What Halvorsen does |
| --- | --- |
| H2 at Moderate or High | Moves 2nd Battalion to Tarsk |
| H2 at Low | Raises readiness but doesn't move |
| H3 (any confidence) | Holds position and leaves it to the diplomats |
| H1 (any confidence) | Relaxes posture and releases the reserve company to training |
| H1 or H3 at Moderate+ after the battalion has moved | Pulls the battalion back to garrison |

## Hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis |

| H2 | **Cover for an operation.** The exercise hides preparations to seize Tarsk Dam. |
| H3 | **Coercion.** A show of force to pressure Elsin at the talks, with no intent to cross. |

## How to play a day (target: about 8 minutes)

1. **Days 2–5:** read Halvorsen's reply to yesterday's brief in Appendix A.
2. Read the inbox.
3. Grade each item yourself: source **A–F**, information **1–6**. Flag anything that doesn't add up. Mark items with nothing to do with the crisis **N/R**.
4. *(Optional)* Mark each item C (consistent), I (inconsistent) or – (neither) against H1–H3.
5. **Brief:** one hypothesis, one confidence level (Low, Moderate or High), up to 3 item IDs as key evidence, and one sentence of reasoning.
6. Write down how long the day took and one line on what was fun or a slog.

## Scoring (add it up at the end, from the key)

Your daily call is scored against **what was actually true that day**.

| Confidence | Right | Wrong |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | +1 | −1 |
| Moderate | +2 | −2 |
| High | +3 | −4 |

Each cited piece of key evidence scores by what it turned out to be: true **+1**, noise **0**, clutter **−1**, planted **−2**. The ending adds a bonus or a penalty.

*Rule of thumb: High pays off if you're about two-thirds sure. Moderate pays off if you're better than even.*
