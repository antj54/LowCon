## Situation (read once)

April. Day 1 is the 14th.

For a year Kordav has kept knowing things it shouldn't: which nets we read, what we'd shared with Kalsa, when. Under Secretary Marlowe has opened a **damage assessment**, and your cell is running it. Her security staff have pulled together what leaked and how each thing travelled. It reaches you a part at a time.

The alliance's traffic with Elsin goes on as usual while the assessment runs: most of it over **LATTICE**, the secure terminal link between the alliance and Elsin's ministries, maintained by Corvane Systems.

**A deadline you know about:** General Halvorsen's revised Veyl contingency plan goes to Kalsa at **0900 on the 17th** (Day 4), by whatever channel is open to it that morning.

## Your customer

**Under Secretary H. Marlowe**, with General Halvorsen copied. Her question every day: *Who or what is reading us, and what do I do about it?*

How she acts on your brief:

| Your brief | What Marlowe does |
| --- | --- |
| H1 at High | Asks Elsin to remove Col. Rhun from the liaison desk. **Can't be undone.** |
| H1 at Medium | Stops sending anything sensitive to Rhun's desk |
| H2 at High | Relieves Maj. Arle, and suspends anyone in your own cell with an open vetting flag pending review. **Can't be undone.** |
| H2 at Medium | Cuts Halvorsen's distribution to named readers |
| H3 at High | Shuts LATTICE. The alliance loses its working link to Elsin for at least a month. **Can't be undone.** |
| H3 at Medium | Moves sensitive traffic to couriers. The link carries routine traffic only. |
| H4 at Medium or High | Closes the assessment: no leak |
| Anything at Low | Nothing |

**If she acts against an ally's colonel or an ally's link, Kalsa will ask what it rests on.** She'd like something to show them.

**Credibility.** It drops if she takes an irreversible step with nothing to show Kalsa yet, if she has to apologise to Kalsa for a step she took on your word, if she has to reopen an assessment she closed, and if you brief Low two days running.

## Collection

**What arrives without asking:** the assessment as it's compiled, the liaison desk, Halvorsen's staff, the vendor and public reporting. **Everything else you task.**

**Budget:** 5 points a day. Most results arrive the next morning. Liaison requests go to Kalsa over LATTICE, unless the link is shut.

**Controlled tests.** You can send a marked item: a notice that Corvane engineers will visit a site on the 24th. Each version names a different site, and only that version names it. Then you watch for the site to turn up on the other side. A test costs 3 points, its result arrives **two days later**, and it can be sent on Days 1 to 3 only. A test over LATTICE can't be sent once the link is shut, including on the day you shut it.

1. **Days 2 onward:** read Marlowe's reply in Appendix A, then your collection results in Appendix C.
2. Read the inbox, any record items from Appendix D, and the analyst notes. Doubt anything you judge false or deliberately misleading, and mark clutter as not relevant.

4. **Brief:** one hypothesis, one confidence level, and up to 3 item IDs as key evidence.
