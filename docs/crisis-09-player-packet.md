## Situation (read once)

The 23rd of October. Kordav's reservists were due home on the 25th. Its note stands: Task Force VEYL out of the Tarsk sector by the 20th of November.

This morning **Teodor Ansel**, Kordav's former ambassador to Pellaria and an old friend of its leadership, asked to see our station chief in Aubrec. He carries an offer. Kordav will end the mobilisation and reopen the water talks. In return the task force withdraws from the Tarsk sector to its garrison for one year, and Elsin guarantees a minimum flow on the Veyl.

He wants an answer in principle by the 28th.

Your cell is with **Under Secretary Marlowe**. Her instructions go to the station in Aubrec each evening.

## Your customer

**Under Secretary H. Marlowe.** Her question: *Is the offer real, and what do I tell him?*

| Your brief | What Marlowe does |
| --- | --- |
| H1 at High | Accepts in principle and sends a negotiator with authority. The pull-back is announced. **Can't be undone.** |
| H1 at Medium | Opens exploratory talks, with no commitments |
| H2 at High | Refuses, publishes the offer, and asks the alliance to reinforce the task force. **Can't be undone.** |
| H2 at Medium | Keeps talking and keeps the task force where it is |
| H3 at High | Flies to Kalsa to agree a joint refusal with Kask |
| H3 at Medium | Briefs Kask's office on every exchange |
| Anything at Low | Nothing. The envoy waits. |

**Credibility** drops if she takes a step that can't be undone with nothing to show for it, if she reverses herself, and if you brief Low two days running.

## What reaches you unasked

Each morning you get, in this order:

1. **STATION's report of the previous day's meeting** with Ansel.
2. **One or two public items** (CWS, KSB).
3. **EMBASSY, Kalsa**, on what Kordav's ambassador is saying to Kask.
4. **An admin note** saying how last night's instruction travelled.
5. Analyst notes, and your collection results.

**Day 1's inbox** is the same for everyone:

> First meeting, Aubrec. Ansel set out the offer as above. He spoke from notes and left no paper.

> Political section: Kordav's ambassador called on Kask yesterday with the same offer, and added a three-year gas price guarantee for Elsin.
