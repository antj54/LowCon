## Situation (read once)

Late May. Day 1 is the 26th.

Elsin's coalition government is in trouble. Prime Minister **Henrik Valde** governs with the **Highlands Party**, a rural party from the north led by **Sanna Ekdal**, and his majority is thin. Ekdal's voters are angry, and so are crowds outside parliament.

The opposition is led by **Ruben Kask** of **National Renewal**, who says Elsin should decide its own future "without Kordav or the alliance." A new government led by Kask would have to decide whether Task Force VEYL stays.

Kordav's broadcaster is enjoying itself.

Your cell supports **Under Secretary H. Marlowe**. The alliance keeps Valde's office informed about Kordavan activity in a short note each evening, drawn from your brief.

## Your customer

**Under Secretary H. Marlowe**, again. Her question every day: *Is this Elsin's business, or Kordav's, and what should the alliance do?*

How she acts on your brief:

| Your brief | What Marlowe does |
| --- | --- |
| H1 at High | Says publicly that the alliance will work with whatever government parliament chooses, and opens talks with the opposition about the task force's future |
| H1 at Medium | Quietly opens a channel to the opposition, through the embassy |
| H2 at High | Asks Valde to postpone the vote until the streets are calm. **Can't be undone.** |
| H2 at Medium | Urges Elsin's police, through the embassy, to show restraint |
| H3 at High | Warns publicly that Kordav is interfering in Elsin's politics. **Can't be undone.** |
| H3 at Medium | Warns Valde privately, in the evening note to his office |
| Anything at Low | Nothing |

**Credibility.** It drops if something she does on your word goes wrong in public, if she has to explain herself to Valde, and if you brief Low two days running.

## How to play a day

1. **Days 2 onward:** read Marlowe's reply in Appendix A, then your collection results in Appendix C, then any items from Appendix D.
2. Read the inbox and the analyst notes. Doubt anything you judge false or deliberately misleading, and mark clutter as not relevant.
3. Task collection for tomorrow.
4. **Brief:** one hypothesis, one confidence level, and up to 3 item IDs as key evidence. Write your key judgment down: the evening note to Valde's office is built from it.
