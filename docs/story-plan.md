*Status: plan on paper, drafted 2026-11-18 (#66, #67). No spoilers about who the leak is: that is in `claude/story-key.md`, which Anthony has asked not to read so he can play the campaign. Built so far: Act 1, and Scenarios 04 and 05 of Act 2 (#70), with the Case file and the three story values recorded. Nothing reads the values yet. Scenario 06 is scripted (#73) and built (#74).*

## The shape

**Ten scenarios: three acts of three, and a finale.** Everyone plays the same scenarios in the same order (a fixed spine). What changes is the state of the world they're played in, which the service record already carries.

Two stories run together:

- **The long game (the plot).** Kordav's campaign to pull Elsin out of the alliance, by force, by subversion and through opportunists like Marrat. It ends back at the Veyl.
- **The leak (the thread).** Kordav keeps knowing what we collect. Across the campaign the player gathers a case file, names a source of the leak, and lives with being right or wrong.

## What the story tracks

Three values on the service record, each moved by scenario outcomes. They sit beside what's already there (credibility with each customer, burned sources, what got past you).

| Value | What it means | What it changes later |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alliance** | How much Elsin trusts the alliance and the task force | Which liaison reporting you get, how much Elsin shares, whether Elsin acts on warnings, and the political scenarios of Act 3 |
| **Pressure** | What Kordav and Marrat hold on the ground and in Kalsa | Where later scenarios start from: who holds the dam, who guards the pipeline, who sits in Vell's chair |
| **Case file** | How much of the leak you've noticed | Which suspects you can rule in or out in Act 2, and whether the finale's reporting can be trusted |

**Case file from Act 1:** each scenario has a moment where the other side knew something it shouldn't have. In 01, the cover story arrived suspiciously soon after our assessment went out. In 02, the Bureau knew which net we read and was watching for whoever asked about LANTERN. In 03, someone knew where the guard company was. Catching the item or running the check that exposes each one adds an entry to the file. A player who noticed none of them starts Act 2 with an empty file and a harder hunt.

**Scenario 06 is the campaign's hinge.** Name the right source at High and the leak is closed before Act 3. Name the wrong one and an innocent career ends while the leak stays open. Hedge and nothing is done. Whatever happens carries into every Act 3 scenario.

## Endings

Two axes, so the ending is the sum of the campaign and not a last-minute choice.

**The world,** from Alliance and Pressure after the finale:

| Ending | In a line |
| --- | --- |
| The alliance holds | Kordav stops at the river. Nobody announces a victory. |
| A cold peace | Elsin stays whole and goes neutral. The task force goes home. |
| Partition | Kordav keeps what it took in the north, and Marrat guards the south. |
| War | The thing the cell existed to prevent. |

**You,** from credibility, the case file and Scenario 06:

| Ending | In a line |
| --- | --- |
| Vindicated | You found the leak and were believed. |
| Trusted, and blind | Your customers relied on you. The leak was never found. |
| The wrong name | You were certain, and wrong, about a colleague. |
| A new cell | Reassigned, with a long record and nobody reading it. |

Each pairing gets a short closing passage: sixteen in all, most of them two or three sentences that differ from their neighbours by a clause.

## What has to be true for this to work

1. **Act 1 outcomes must bite.** Today, losing the dam changes nothing later except Halvorsen's mood. Act 2 openings and Act 3 starting positions have to read the three values.
2. **The leak needs clues in Act 1.** They are already in the fiction. They need marking so the record can count them, and a line in each postmortem: "An open question: how did they know?"
3. **The leak's identity is fixed.** One truth, authored, with a tell and cross-checks like any other deception. Which clues you see depends on what you collected and noticed.
4. **A case file screen** on the main menu, beside the service record: what you've noticed, in plain words, with no conclusions drawn for you.
5. **Suspects are introduced before they're suspected.** Act 2 needs a small supporting cast who have been on the page since earlier scenarios: people in Elsin's service, on Halvorsen's staff and in Marlowe's office, your own analysts, and the possibility that it isn't a person at all but a compromised channel.

## Critic's notes

- **Seven more scenarios is the largest writing job in the project.** Each one is about the size of The Southern Line.
- **Nothing in Act 1 has been played by a tester in its current form.** Building seven scenarios on it before one campaign playtest is the biggest risk in the plan.
- **A fixed leak means a second playthrough knows the answer.** That's acceptable: the same is true of every scenario, and the record's first-finish rule already treats replays as practice.
- **Scenario 08 uses the adversary-reacts idea from the parking lot.** It belongs here and not earlier.
- **Ten weeks of daily play is long.** The act breaks should feel like endings, each with its own short epilogue from the record.
