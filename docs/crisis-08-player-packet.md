## Situation (read once)

The 2nd of October, a Monday. Two years after IRON HARVEST.

Kordav has announced IRON HARVEST again. This year it has also called up 40,000 reservists, cancelled leave across 4th Army, and moved a second army's headquarters to Ostrel. Its defence minister says the exercise will run "until the security of the Veyl is assured."

Elsin's government under Ruben Kask is four months old. Elsin can call up its own reserves. Doing so stops the harvest and most of the economy.

Your cell is back with General Halvorsen. Each evening the task force sends Kalsa a short assessment drawn from your brief.

## Your customer

**Gen. R. Halvorsen.** His question: *Is this an attack, and what do I do with the task force?*

| Your brief | What Halvorsen does |
| --- | --- |
| H1 at High | Moves the task force into forward positions, asks the alliance for reinforcement, and advises Kask to call up Elsin's reserves. **The call-up can't be undone.** |
| H1 at Medium | Moves into forward positions quietly |
| H2 at High | Stands fast, and tells Kask in person that no attack is coming and not to call up |
| H2 at Medium | Holds his posture, and tells Kask's office privately |
| H3 at High | Moves the task force south to the Southern Line |
| H3 at Medium | Sends one company south |
| Anything at Low | Nothing |

**Kask will ask what it rests on.** Halvorsen would like something to show him. Kask decides on a call-up on the 6th, with or without the alliance's advice.

**Credibility** drops if Halvorsen has to turn troops round, if he advises Kask with nothing to show, and if you brief Low two days running.

## Day 1: Monday the 2nd

*Read-in:* Kordav has called up 40,000 reservists for IRON HARVEST.

**R-1-01** · CWS · **B1**
> From Kordav's capital: the defence ministry has called up 40,000 reservists "for the duration of IRON HARVEST." Call-up notices give a reporting date of the 4th. Leave is cancelled across 4th Army.

> *Marrow:* Notices with a reporting date. I'd look for a release date on them too.

**R-1-02** · KSB · **D1**
> State television, evening news. The defence minister: "The Veyl is Kordav's river too. We will exercise until its security is assured."

> *Vasko:* He said much the same two years ago, and I called it theatre. Then they went for the dam. I'd plan for a crossing.

**R-1-03** · WREN · **B3**
> From the liaison desk: "Our assessment is that this is the attack. We ask the alliance to reinforce at once."
> Sourcing: none given.

**R-1-04** · EMBASSY · **B2**
> Political section: Kask's office asks for the alliance's assessment. *[B2]*

**R-1-05** · Task force admin · **A1**
